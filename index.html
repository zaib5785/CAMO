The camera is black or won't open

CAMO needs camera access. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Apps, then CAMO, and turn on Camera.

Where are my photos?

Tap the photo in the bottom-left corner of the camera to open your CAMO gallery. Every shot is also saved to your camera roll.

Can I change a look after taking a photo?

Yes. CAMO keeps the original of every photo. Open it in the gallery and tap Edit look. Press and hold a photo to see the original.

How do I record a video?

Press and hold the shutter button. Clips can be up to 15 seconds.

How do I share a look with a friend?

Press and hold a look you made or copied, then tap Share look. Your friend can scan the QR code with CAMO or their iPhone camera.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

CAMO is a camera app made by Zaib Qureshi. This policy explains what CAMO does with your information. The short version: CAMO does not collect any data.

Your photos stay on your phone

Photos, videos and looks you create are stored only on your device. CAMO does not upload them anywhere.

No accounts, ads or tracking

CAMO has no sign-in, shows no ads, and does not use analytics or tracking tools. It does not share information with anyone.

Permissions CAMO asks for

  • Camera: to show the live camera and take photos and videos.
  • Microphone: only while you hold the shutter to record a video.
  • Photos: to save your shots to your camera roll, and to open photos you pick yourself (to copy a look or bring them into CAMO).

You can turn any of these off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it, for example sending a photo or a look's QR code to a friend. What happens after that is up to the app you share with.

Children

CAMO does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
